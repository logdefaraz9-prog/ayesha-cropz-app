Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Ayesha Cropz website and mobile app ("we", "us") collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Ayesha Cropz, Ayesha Business Center, At Post & Tal Mhasla, Opp HP Petrol Pump, Mhasla, Dist. Raigad – 402105, Maharashtra, India. Email: info@ayeshacropz.co.in. Phone: +91 76665 89729.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to type into our forms:

  • Inquiry form: your name, phone number, email address (optional), the division you are interested in, and your message.
  • Careers form: your name, phone number, email address (optional), the team you are interested in, your experience level and a short note about yourself.
  • Order form: your name, mobile number, delivery address (if you choose delivery), the items you order and your chosen payment method. If you pay by UPI you may also give us your UPI reference number.

The app does not use advertising, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not collect your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

3. How we use your information

We use the information only to reply to your inquiry, to consider your job application, and to contact you about the products and services you asked about.

4. How your form is delivered

When you submit a form, the details are sent by email to our inbox at info@ayeshacropz.co.in. This delivery is handled by a form-processing service (FormSubmit, formsubmit.co) that passes your submission to us. It acts on our behalf to deliver the message and is subject to its own privacy terms.

5. Other services

  • Google Sheets: orders are saved in our own Google Sheet, which only our team can open, and the app reads the stock numbers from it.
  • Order tracking: to show your order status we look up your order by order ID and mobile number. Your recent order IDs are kept on your own device so you can check them again.
  • UPI payments: payments are made in your own UPI app. We never see your bank login or card details.
  • Google Fonts: when you are online, the app and website load fonts from Google, which may receive your IP address for that purpose.
  • Assistant: the “Ask Ayesha” assistant answers from information stored in the app and does not send your questions to any server. If an online AI service is added in future, this policy will be updated first.
  • Calls, email and WhatsApp: links such as “Call”, “Email” and “WhatsApp” open other apps (your phone, email or WhatsApp). Those apps are governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers described above to operate the forms, or when the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep inquiries and applications for as long as needed to respond to you or to consider you for a role, and then delete them when they are no longer needed.

8. Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you sent us by emailing info@ayeshacropz.co.in. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Security

Submissions are sent over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Our app and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Job applicants must be of legal working age.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@ayeshacropz.co.in or call +91 76665 89729.
